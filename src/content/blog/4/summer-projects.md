As a side-quest to this whole self hosting Server build, I remembered I have an old burnt out PSU lying around. "*Why not try to fix it? It'll be good HW repair experience!*" I thought. So with no idea where to start, I set out to figure it out. 

Thankfully, [Reddit was a lot of help](https://www.reddit.com/r/ElectronicsRepair/comments/1uqz15b/help_repairing_a_seasonic_ssr650gb_s12iii650/). I now have some next steps to take and I'm just, again, waiting to finish the Switch Review video.
