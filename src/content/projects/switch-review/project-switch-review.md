## The Task

I was was invited to a switch testing program by a Keyboard and Switch manufacturer: Akko

Akko is releasing a new line of switches the U1 Switches. They come in 4 different types: 

- Creamy Purple U1 Tactile
- Creamy Yellow U1 Linear
- Crystal Silver U1 Linear
- Piano U1 Linear

They asked me to pick one of these so they could send them over for review.

I ended up choosing the Cystal Silver U1 as the Silver line of switches has been one of my favourites over the years.

## The Deal

The only thing they asked in return was a video review of the switches they have sent me with an unboxing, switch showcase and typing test.
