Project: Keyboard Switches Review

tech-reviewvideo-makingkeyboard-switchesvideo-editingAI

The Task

I was invited to a switch testing program by a Keyboard and Switch manufacturer: Akko

Akko is releasing a new line of switches the U1 Switches. They come in 4 different types:

  • Creamy Purple U1 Tactile
  • Creamy Yellow U1 Linear
  • Crystal Silver U1 Linear
  • Piano U1 Linear

They asked me to pick one of these so they could send them over for review.

I ended up choosing the Crystal Silver U1 as the Silver line of switches has been one of my favourites over the years.

The Deal

The only thing they asked in return was a video review of the switches they have sent me with an unboxing, switch showcase and typing test.

If you want to know how I ended up doing this with no experience, check out this blog post.
